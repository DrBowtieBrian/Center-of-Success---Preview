# Field history — CircleUp! facilitation record

Compiled 2026-07-29 from Otter.ai recordings and Google Drive, covering
2025-01-01 to present.

**What this document is.** A record that this work has been practiced, not theorized:
dates, sessions, duration, and the arc of the material. It is the evidence base for
"proven inside Symba" (Constitution §6).

**What this document deliberately is not.** It contains no participant names, no
disclosures, no personal content from any session. Several of these recordings
contain staff describing isolation, fear, exhaustion, and family circumstance, along
with prayer offered for named individuals. That material is exactly what
Constitution §2 forbids turning into a data point, and a git repository is not where
it belongs. The transcripts remain where they are, under the access controls they
already have. What is captured here is the *facilitation* record — the work Brian did,
not what participants confided.

## What this changes about the build

**1. The pilot cohort already exists.** I had this listed as an open decision. It is
not open: three standing groups at VV Wellness Center, twelve weeks in, with a
monthly all-campus circle above them. The schema's cohort-first design was correct,
and the seed data should model A/B/C rather than invented groups.

**2. Facilitators are a first-class role, not an afterthought.** The rhythm is
facilitator-led with debriefs afterward. Debriefs are a distinct artifact and the
data model should hold them.

**3. The app's CircleUp screen does not match the actual protocol.** Per
*CircleUp Formation Standard v1.0* in Drive, a faithful session has six movements —
**Center → Surface → Name → Mirror → Practice → Return**. The app currently shows a
generic four-step session structure I invented. That should be replaced with the
real one.

**4. Vices are missing from the model.** Overextended skills becoming vices, corrected
by the adjacent skill, has been taught since November 2025 and appears nowhere in the
app. The Compass is currently drawn as virtues only.

**5. There is already a ratified Constitution.** `Center of Success Constitution v1.0`
(2026-07-24) sits in a `Center of Success Constitutional System` folder alongside
Canons of Practice, a Field Guide, a Governance Charter, and the CircleUp Formation
Standard. The `docs/CONSTITUTION.md` I wrote in this repository was composed without
knowledge of it and must be subordinated to the ratified document rather than
competing with it.
