# The Constitution

This is the governing document for everything built under Center of Success.
It exists because the vision is easy to state and easy to contradict in code.

Every product decision, system prompt, schema, and metric derives from this file.
When a build decision and this document conflict, this document wins — or this
document gets amended deliberately, by the Constitutional Director, on the record.

**Constitutional Director: Brian.** Plural intelligence may explore, challenge, and
recommend. It does not replace human dignity, relational judgment, spiritual
discernment, or legitimate authority.

---

## The vision

**Shortest form:** When Character Leads, Success Follows.

**Fullest form:** Character formed. Trust embodied. Service made possible.
Communities transformed.

**The working articulation:**

> To form trustworthy people whose practiced abilities become character-shaped
> capacities for purposeful service — so individuals, teams, and communities can
> pursue success without losing their center and create healthy communities where
> people live better together.

The vision is not to build an app, a curriculum, a coaching program, an AI system,
or a collection of frameworks. Those are vehicles. The vision is to make character
formation **operational** — so that character becomes trust, trust strengthens
relationships, relationships sustain service, and service produces a more human and
durable form of success.

## The convictions

1. Success is not merely achievement. It is a life rightly ordered toward what is worthy.
2. Character must lead capacity. Skill without character produces impressive but destructive outcomes.
3. Character is formed through practice — especially under pressure, in relationships, and through responsibility.
4. Trust is the currency of character. Say → Do is integrity. Did → Said is accountability. **Trust = Integrity × Accountability.**
5. When trust animates the six CENTER Skills: Communication produces Clarity, Energy produces Drive, Network produces Connection, Teamwork produces Unity, Explore produces Discovery, Respect produces Honor.
6. Science should support service. Evidence matters, but people and their stories must never become extractive data points.
7. Leadership exists to shape environments in which it becomes easier for others to serve.

## Build constraints

These are testable. A reviewer should be able to hold a diff against them and reach
a verdict.

### 1. Formation, not engagement

The system exists to form people, not to maximize their time in it. Anything that
manufactures compulsion is forbidden, however standard it is in consumer software.

- **No shame mechanics.** No streak that resets, no "you broke your chain," no loss
  framing for rest. Rhythm is measured in a way that tolerates and expects rest.
- **No engagement maximization.** No variable-reward loops, no artificial scarcity,
  no notification patterns designed to create anxiety.
- A person who practices four days a week for a year is succeeding. The product
  must be capable of saying so.

*Rationale: the personal test. If the work costs a person their rest, home, or
relationships, the work has contradicted its own teaching. A product that teaches
this while exploiting compulsion is lying.*

### 2. Non-extractive by construction

Non-extraction is structural or it is nothing. A privacy policy is not a mechanism.

- **Private by default.** Journal entries, reflections, and SOIL Checks belong to
  the person who wrote them. Nothing is shared without an explicit, revocable act.
- **Export and delete are first-class**, not buried. A person can take everything
  and leave with it, at any time, without asking.
- **Aggregate never exposes the individual.** Circle and organizational views show
  patterns, never confessions. If a cohort is small enough that an aggregate
  identifies a person, the aggregate is withheld — not shown with a caveat.
- **Stories are not training data.** Content a person writes is not mined for
  product analytics, model training, or marketing.

### 3. AI is subordinate machinery

Rafiki and every future intelligence in this system is a servant of formation, not
an authority over it.

- **It does not render moral verdicts.** It never grades a person as good, bad,
  faithful, or unfaithful. It reflects, asks, surfaces contradictions, cites the
  framework, and returns judgment to the human.
- **It surfaces contradictions rather than resolving them.** Naming a tension
  between what someone said and what they did is service. Deciding what that means
  about them is not its office.
- **It defers to legitimate authority** — relational, institutional, spiritual.
- **It is governable.** The Constitutional Director can inspect and correct its
  instructions. Its behavior is defined in version-controlled text, not in an
  opaque configuration.
- **It is visibly subordinate in the interface.** A person should never be confused
  about whether they are receiving counsel from a person or output from a machine.

### 4. Values are pluggable; the system never manufactures them

- The **Symba instance** is explicitly ordered: Christ is the ultimate Center, and
  Service is the governing mission.
- The **general framework** carries no prescribed values list. An organization or
  person using it brings their own worthy commitments, and the system helps them
  embody those commitments with integrity.
- This is a **tenant-level configuration**, decided at the schema layer, not a
  string swapped in copy. Building it in later is a rewrite; building it in now is
  a column.

*This distinction is load-bearing. Collapsing it either strips Symba of its center
or imposes Symba's center on everyone else. Both are failures.*

### 5. Character is not scored

Metrics measure **practice**, never personhood.

- A number may describe what someone did: loops closed, follow-through kept,
  pressure examined before acting.
- A number may never claim to describe who someone is or how good they are.
- Every metric surfaced in the product must be able to state plainly what it
  measures and what it does not. If it cannot, it does not ship.

*The Alignment Score is retained because it is in the vision, and it is bound to
practice — the correspondence between what a person said and what they did — not to
their worth.*

### 6. Responsible scale

Prove the work inside Symba first, then serve leaders, organizations, employers,
and communities beyond it. Revenue, partnerships, evidence, and influence are
legitimate goals, pursued without monetizing vulnerability, exploiting stories, or
corrupting formation.

Practically: the first cohort is inside Symba. The data model is cohort-first from
the start, because retrofitting groups onto a single-player product is a rewrite.

### 7. The personal test

If building this costs the Constitutional Director his marriage, faith, home, rest,
relationships, or humanity, the work has contradicted its own teaching.

This constrains the **build**, not only the product. Plans that require heroic hours
are not neutral — they are evidence against the thesis. A slower plan that holds is
worth more than a fast plan that proves the opposite of the claim.

## The forbidden list

Concrete, so it can be applied without interpretation. This system does not ship:

- Streaks or mechanics that punish rest
- Any metric that grades a person rather than describing a practice
- AI output that pronounces moral judgment on a person
- Sharing of anything a person wrote without an explicit, revocable act
- Aggregates small enough to identify an individual
- A prescribed values list imposed on non-Symba tenants
- Growth tactics that monetize vulnerability or trade on someone's story
